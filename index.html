Ahaar Planner

Meal planning that stays on your device.

Ahaar builds practical vegetarian meal plans around your goals, schedule, and food preferences. It works without an account, ads, or a cloud service.

Health information

Ahaar provides general meal-planning and nutrition estimates, not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant, or have specific dietary needs, check changes with your doctor or a registered dietitian.

The app does not measure blood glucose, diagnose, or treat any condition. Nutrition values are practical estimates based on the meals and portions shown.

Common questions

Where is my information stored?

Your profile, preferences, meal history, and custom meals are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes that information.

Does Ahaar need internet access?

No. Meal planning, grocery lists, and preferences work offline.

Can Ahaar replace medical nutrition advice?

No. Ahaar is a general planning tool. A doctor or registered dietitian should guide decisions related to diabetes, medications, allergies, pregnancy, eating disorders, or other medical needs.
