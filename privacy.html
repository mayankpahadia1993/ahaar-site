Ahaar Planner

Privacy Policy

Ahaar is an offline meal planner. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud service, and it does not collect personal information.

Information stored on your device

Ahaar stores the information you enter—such as weight, planning goal, food preferences, avoided ingredients, meal ratings, custom meals, meal history, and grocery-list state—locally on your device so the app can work.

This information is not transmitted to us or to a third party. Deleting the app deletes its locally stored information.

Information collected

None. Ahaar does not collect names, email addresses, health records, identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, advertising data, or diagnostic data.

Sharing and tracking

Ahaar does not sell or share data, use third-party advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or create user profiles for advertising.

Children

Ahaar is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@mysticoai.com.
